My Roseantic,

You know what was hard about making this little surprise? You’re the first person I wanted to send a picture to and ask, “What do you think?” Even when the secret is about you, you’re the person I want to tell.

I hope this year gives you a reason to call me breathless and say, “Guess what?” And on the days when the only news is what you had for lunch, call me then too. I want all of it with you.

If I could reach through this screen, I’d give you the first bite of cake and pull you into the kind of hug that makes us forget there’s a whole cake waiting.

Happy birthday, my love. I’m so glad it’s you.

Always yours,Punith
